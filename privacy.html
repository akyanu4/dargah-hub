Privacy

Draft. Last updated 12 October 2026. Review before launch.

In short: no accounts, no tracking, no ads network, and nothing is sold. This site only reads public listings.

What we collect

  • Your city, calculation settings, tasbeeh count and daily prayer marks are saved only on your phone. If you tap "Use my location", your position is used on the phone to work out prayer times and Qibla and is not sent to us.
  • Nothing about you is stored on our servers. It keeps a copy of its pages on your device so it can open faster.
  • If you email us to list a dargah, shop or ad, we keep your message and contact details to reply and to run the listing.

What leaves your device

  • Hosting is on Vercel, which keeps ordinary server logs (such as IP address).
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts, which may see your IP address as any website would.
  • Links to vendors, maps and videos open other websites with their own privacy rules. Purchases happen on the vendor's side, not here.

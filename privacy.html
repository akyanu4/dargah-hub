Privacy

Draft. Last updated 17 October 2026. Have a lawyer review it before launch.

In short: buyers do not need an account, there is no tracking and no ads network, and nothing is sold. We keep only what is needed to pass your order or message to the right person.

What we collect

  • Your city, calculation settings, tasbeeh count and daily prayer marks are saved only on your phone. If you tap "Use my location", your position is used on the phone to work out prayer times and Qibla and is not sent to us.
  • Your cart, your list of past orders and your name, phone and address (saved after you place an order, to fill the form next time) stay on your device. Clear your browser data to remove them.
  • If you send a form to list a dargah, advertise, contact us or report something, we keep what you wrote and your contact details to reply and to run the listing.

What leaves your device

  • Hosting is on Vercel, which keeps ordinary server logs (such as IP address).
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts, which may see your IP address as any website would.
  • Links to vendors, maps and videos open other websites with their own privacy rules. Payment and delivery are handled by the vendor.

Orders

  • When you send an order request we store your name, phone number, delivery address, note and the items ordered, using Google Firebase.
  • The vendor you order from can see these details, and so can the Dargah Hub team (to help with complaints). Nobody else can list or browse orders. Anyone who has your order link can open that one order, so do not share it.
  • We do not take payment and do not see your payment details. You pay the vendor directly.
  • We keep order records for as long as needed to handle complaints and for legal and tax reasons, then delete them. Ask us to delete yours earlier at the address below.

Visitor accounts (optional)

  • You do not need an account to use Dargah Hub. If you create one (email and password, or Google), we store your sign-in email and a small record of the dargahs and shops you follow, the items you saved and your saved deal codes, using Google Firebase, so they follow you to a new phone.
  • Only you can read or change this record. You can delete it from the Account page at any time. To close your sign-in itself, write to us using the contact on the Contact page.
  • Your prayer settings, location and tasbeeh count still stay only on your phone.

Vendor accounts

  • If you sell on the hub, we store your sign-in email, business details (name, city, description, links, WhatsApp and phone), your items and the photos you upload, using Google Firebase.
  • Approved vendor details and items are public. Your sign-in email is not shown.
  • You can edit or delete your items at any time, and email us to delete your account.

Your choices

  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your order details or your messages by writing to us.
  • Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act you can also complain to us first, using the grievance contact in the app under More, Contact and complaints.

Terms for vendors, dargahs and advertisers

Draft for discussion. Have a lawyer review it before you rely on it.

The platform

  • Dargah Hub lists dargahs, vendors and products. It is not the seller and does not handle payment, delivery, returns or refunds.
  • Every listing is approved by hand and may be removed for breaking these terms.

Vendors

  • You are the seller. You handle orders, delivery, returns, refunds, complaints and any tax.
  • Products must be genuine and described honestly (for example, say whether an attar is alcohol-free). Photos and text must be yours or used with permission.
  • A yearly listing fee applies. Fees are not refunded for removal caused by breaking these terms.
  • Paid placement is always labelled Sponsored.

Dargahs

  • Pages are published only with the authorised keeper's consent. Content (photos, poetry, recordings) must be owned by the dargah or used with permission.
  • We do not judge between rival claims. If a page is disputed we may pause it until the dispute is settled.

Advertisers

  • Ads must be lawful, respectful and suitable for a religious community. We may refuse or remove any ad.
  • Ads never appear on Qur'an or prayer content.

Liability

The platform is provided as is. We are not responsible for vendor goods or services. Local consumer and tax laws apply to sellers.

Terms for vendors, dargahs and advertisers

Draft. Last updated 17 October 2026. Have a lawyer review it before you rely on it.

The platform

  • Dargah Hub lists dargahs, vendors and products and passes buyers' order requests to vendors. It is a listing platform. It is not the seller and does not take payment or handle delivery, returns or refunds.
  • An order request is not a contract of sale until the vendor confirms it. Price, availability and delivery charges are confirmed by the vendor.
  • Every listing is approved by hand and may be removed for breaking these terms.

Vendors

  • You are the seller. You handle orders, delivery, returns, refunds, complaints and any tax, including GST where it applies to you.
  • Answer order requests promptly: confirm or cancel within 2 days. Keep your order status up to date in your vendor page.
  • Show your real name or business name and contact details. Do not collect buyers' payment through us, and use buyers' details only to fulfil their order.
  • Do not list: alcohol-based perfumes sold as alcohol-free, food without correct labelling, counterfeit or stolen goods, medicines or anything unlawful or not halal.
  • Products must be genuine and described honestly (for example, say whether an attar is alcohol-free). Photos and text must be yours or used with permission.
  • A yearly listing fee applies. Fees are not refunded for removal caused by breaking these terms.
  • Paid placement is always labelled Sponsored.

Vendor accounts

  • You may list only goods and services that are halal and lawful where you sell.
  • You are responsible for what you upload and for keeping your sign-in safe.
  • We may pause or remove a vendor or item at any time, for example for misleading descriptions or complaints.

Dargahs

  • Pages are published only with the authorised keeper's consent. Content (photos, poetry, recordings) must be owned by the dargah or used with permission.
  • We do not judge between rival claims. If a page is disputed we may pause it until the dispute is settled.

Advertisers

  • Ads must be lawful, respectful and suitable for a religious community. We may refuse or remove any ad.
  • Ads never appear on Qur'an or prayer content.

Buyers

  • Send order requests only if you intend to buy. Give a real name, phone number and address.
  • You pay the vendor directly after they confirm. Check the product, price and return terms with the vendor first.
  • If something goes wrong, contact the vendor first, then use Contact and complaints in the app and we will help to settle it.

Reports and complaints

  • Anyone can report an item, vendor, dargah page or order. We review reports and may hide or remove a listing.
  • Grievance contact: Dargah Hub team (see More, Contact and complaints). We acknowledge complaints within 48 hours and aim to resolve them within 30 days.

Liability

The platform is provided as is. We are not responsible for vendor goods or services. Local consumer and tax laws apply to sellers.
